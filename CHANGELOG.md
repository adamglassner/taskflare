# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First release of Taskflare, a free and open-source to-do list for Windows 10 and 11 by Bytehawk Studio.

- My Day, Important, Planned (grouped into Earlier, Today, Tomorrow, This week and Later) and Tasks, like Microsoft To Do, plus your own lists with a colour and an icon. Unlimited tasks and lists.
- Todoist-style quick add: type "Call mom tomorrow 5pm #Personal p1" and Taskflare sets the date, time, list and priority. Also understands "every monday", "next week", "in 3 days", "nov 20", @labels and * for important.
- Details pane for each task: steps, My Day, reminders, due date and time, repeat, priority, list, labels and notes.
- Reminders pop up in the corner of the screen with Complete and Snooze, even when the window is closed (Taskflare keeps running in the notification area).
- Repeating tasks (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly), priorities, stars, sorting, search, drag to reorder and drag onto a list to move.
- Lightbulb suggestions for My Day: overdue, due soon and recently added tasks.
- Muse AI connector: lets Meta's Muse add, read and tick off your tasks. Built-in MCP server and JSON API with a secret key, plus a one-click public link through a free Cloudflare quick tunnel.
- Light and Dark mode (or follow Windows) with six accent colours.
- Saved on your PC with a daily backup; export and import. Every task has the ids and timestamps a future mobile sync needs.
- Installer (with a "just for me" option) and a portable zip. Available in the Bytehawk Studio Launcher.
